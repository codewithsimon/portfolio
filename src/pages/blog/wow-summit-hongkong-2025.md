## My Session: Advanced – Cost-Effective Generative AI with Azure AI Foundry

I presented an advanced-level session titled:
**“Cost-Effective Generative AI: Optimizing Workflows on Azure AI Foundry.”**

Generative AI has transformed industries, but one of the biggest challenges that organizations face is cost management. Deploying large models, running workloads at scale, and handling unpredictable usage can quickly lead to budget concerns. My session focused on practical strategies to tackle exactly this challenge.

Here’s what I covered:

- **Model Selection:** Choosing the right-sized model for the task, instead of defaulting to the largest available option.

- **Caching Strategies:** Reducing redundant calls to models with smart caching layers.

- **Token-Efficient Prompts:** Designing prompt structures that cut down token usage without compromising output quality.

- **Load Balancing:** Optimizing workloads across endpoints and regions for efficiency and reliability.

- **Leveraging Open-Source Models:** Running fine-tuned open-source models inside Azure AI Foundry to balance performance with cost.

The audience was a mix of technical practitioners, business leaders, and researchers—many of whom resonated with the need to balance innovation with sustainability. The Q&A session was particularly engaging, with questions around real-world case studies and implementation strategies in enterprise environments.
